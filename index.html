Who We Are

Welcome to Palmetto Card Co. — where collecting meets community. Based in South Carolina, we're passionate about trading cards, collectibles, and the thrill of the pull. Whether you're chasing rare Pokémon hits, building your personal collection, or just getting started, we're here to make the experience fun, fair, and exciting. At Palmetto Card Co., we specialize in live breaks, curated packs, and authentic collectibles you can trust. Our goal is simple: bring transparency, energy, and a hometown vibe to the hobby. Every stream, every pack, and every order is handled with care—because we're collectors too.
